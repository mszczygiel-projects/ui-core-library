# Theming & branding

A consumer brands the library on three tiers. The first one is enough to brand everything; the other two refine it.

1. **Brand.** One to three colour ramps and about twenty brand values: the fonts, the radii, flags such as capitals on buttons or underlined links, and the geometry of the focus ring.
2. **Roles.** Which step of a ramp a role takes, the typography scale and the density, in their own copy of the Foundations file.
3. **Components.** Any change to one component, through its own tokens.

## Brand values

The brand values are roles of the Themes collection that hold no colour, such as `--font-heading`, `--shape-radius-md` or `--text-uppercase-button`. They are declared once on `:root`, in the cascade layer `ui-core-tokens`. Override them outside any layer, and every role and component follows:

```css
:root {
  --font-heading: 'Barlow Condensed', sans-serif;
  --shape-radius-md: 0;
  --text-uppercase-button: none;
}
```

[View brand values](?path=/story/foundations-theming-branding--brand-values)

The brand colour ramps, `--color-brand-primary-50` to `--color-brand-tertiary-1000`, are overridden the same way; [Colors](?path=/docs/foundations-colors--docs) shows them. Roles are declared again for every theme and surface, so they change through a build of a consumer's own export instead.

## Component tokens

The tokens of a component are its styling API. A token that no mode changes, such as `--button-radius`, is declared once on `:root` and overridden there, like a brand value. Any other token is set on the components themselves, through a selector that matches them. A value that points at a role resolves on the component, so it keeps following the theme and the surface.

## Several brands

A copy of the Foundations file may hold several brands as modes of its Themes collection: `Club Light` and `Club Dark`, or `Club` alone. `data-brand` on `<html>` chooses the brand and `data-theme` its scheme. The roles are declared on the root element, so a brand set further down does not reach them.

```html
<html data-brand="club" data-theme="dark"></html>
```

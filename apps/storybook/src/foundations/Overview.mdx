# Foundations

The design tokens are the values every component of the library is built from: colours, typography, spacing, shapes, shadows and motion. Designers keep them as variables in the [Core] Foundations Figma file, and `@mszczygiel-projects/ui-core-foundations` turns them into CSS custom properties, a Tailwind theme and typed references.

## Layers

Tokens point at each other in three layers, from raw values to components:

1. **Primitives** are the raw ramps and scales, such as `--color-gray-500` or `--spacing-4`. The brand ramps, `--color-brand-*`, are primitives a consumer replaces to brand the library.
2. **Roles** say what a value is for, such as `--color-text-primary` or `--typography-body-font-size`, and follow the theme, the surface, the screen width and the density. The brand values, such as `--font-body` or `--shape-radius-md`, are roles too; see [Theming & branding](?path=/docs/foundations-theming-branding--docs).
3. **Components** hold the tokens of one component, such as `--button-padding-inline`, and of the family of controls, such as `--control-font-size`.

Choose roles when values should follow the brand, theme, surface or density. Choose primitives for deliberate fixed values; they are available in code and the appropriate Figma property pickers. For example, Tailwind’s `p-4` keeps its spacing when density changes. These pages focus on roles, with the brand ramps and motion primitives also shown.

Library components keep their own token layer. An approved geometry constant can point at a specific primitive: `--loader-stroke-width` stays 2 px through `--border-width-2`, independently of the shared accent stroke. Colour, typography, radii and mode-dependent values still go through roles. Shared `control/*` tokens remain shared until a component needs independent customization.

## Themes or Sizes

The roles live in three collections, and the rule of where a role belongs has no exceptions:

- **Sizes** holds everything that changes with the width of the screen or with the density: type sizes and line heights, gaps, padding, icon sizes, heights and the layout of the page. Its four modes are Mobile, Desktop, Mobile Compact and Desktop Compact.
- **Themes** holds the rest: the colour roles, the fonts, weights, letter spacing and capitals, the radii, strokes and shadows, and the focus ring. Its modes are the themes, and in a consumer's copy the brands.
- **Surfaces** holds the colour roles once more for each surface.

Components take both through their own tokens. The layout roles, `--layout-*`, space the page and its regions, not the inside of a component.

## Using the tokens

```css
@import '@mszczygiel-projects/ui-core-foundations/tokens.css';
@import '@mszczygiel-projects/ui-core-foundations/typography.css';
```

Every token is a custom property, such as `var(--color-text-primary)`, and every text style a class, such as `ui-text-body`. With Tailwind 4, import `tailwind.css` after Tailwind instead of `tokens.css`. `tokens.json` describes every token with its collection, description and values in every mode.

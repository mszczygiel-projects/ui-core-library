# Colors

Colours reach the interface through **roles**: tokens named after what they paint, such as `--color-text-primary` or `--color-border-default`. A role takes its value from the theme and from the surface it sits on, so the same markup reads well in the light and the dark theme and on every surface. Write roles in your styles.

## Roles

Each role in the light and the dark theme, on the default surface. [Surfaces & themes](?path=/docs/foundations-surfaces-themes--docs) shows how the other surfaces change them.

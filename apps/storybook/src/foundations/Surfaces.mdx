# Surfaces & themes

Two attributes decide the colours of an element and of everything inside it. `data-theme` chooses the theme, light or dark, and goes on `<html>`; without it, the page follows the system setting. `data-surface` marks a region painted in another tone: `subtle` for a quiet panel, `inverse` for the opposite tone of the page and `primary` for the brand colour. On a surface, every colour role switches to the variant that reads well there, so a text, a border or a button inside it needs no colours of its own.

```html
<html data-theme="dark">
  <section data-surface="inverse">…</section>
</html>
```

Set `data-surface` on an element inside the one that sets `data-theme`, never on the same element, and never on `<html>`: on one element, the colours of the surface point back at themselves and the browser drops them. `data-surface="default"` returns a region inside another surface to the page's own colours.

# Status of the components

Every component of the library, by category, with where it stands. A component is **planned** until its work starts, **in-progress** while it is built, and **done** when both implementations meet the Definition of Done. The status of a component comes from its specification in `@mszczygiel-projects/ui-core-shared`.

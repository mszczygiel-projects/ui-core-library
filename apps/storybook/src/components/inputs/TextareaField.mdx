# TextareaField

<ComponentOverview name="TextareaField" />

## Usage

Use TextareaField for text of more than one line, such as a message or a description. Use TextField for a single line.

## Anatomy

The **base** holds the **label**, **control** and **hint**, as in TextField. The control holds the native textarea, the **input** part. The web component exposes these parts; React uses `.ui-textarea-field__<part>` classes. The Figma component set has the properties and layers of TextField without its icons.

## Variants & tone

<FrameworkCanvas react={ReactStories.Variants} webComponents={WebComponentStories.Variants} />

`outline` is the default, `filled` adds a filled surface and `underlined` keeps only the bottom border, as in TextField. There is no tone.

## Sizes & density

`small`, `medium` (the default) and `large` follow the sizes of TextField. The control keeps the designed minimum height of its size, `textarea/min-height`, in each density.

## States

`state` is `default`, `error` or `success`, as in TextField. Error sets `aria-invalid` and the feedback colours; it does not create a native validation failure. Hover and press share the active styling, keyboard focus uses the focus roles, and disabled fields leave the tab order and submit nothing. Read-only fields stay focusable and submit their value.

## Content & labels

Supply `label`, `placeholder` and `hint` in the language of the page. Say a limit in the hint and set `maxLength`; the field shows no counter.

## Behaviour

<FrameworkCanvas react={ReactStories.Resize} webComponents={WebComponentStories.Resize} />

- Enter starts a new line and never submits the form.
- `resize="vertical"` (the default) lets the user drag the height. `none` keeps the designed height. `auto` grows with the content and with a narrower page, up to `maxRows` lines, and then scrolls.
- The value, reset, restore and validity (`required`, `minLength`, `maxLength`) follow TextField.

## Accessibility

- **Pattern.** A native multi-line textbox with its label and hint; no composite pattern applies.
- **Keys.** The native keys of a textarea; Tab leaves it.
- **Preferences.** Forced colours keep the border and the focus indicator; the label motion follows reduced motion.

## Surfaces & themes

The field follows Light and Dark and the default, subtle, inverse and primary surfaces with the `control` family, as TextField does.

React's className, style and data-\* attributes reach the root; its ref, id, ARIA and native attributes reach the textarea. The web component uses the attributes `resize`, `max-rows`, `label-placement`, `min-length`, `max-length`, `auto-complete` and `readonly`, and passes the native `inputmode`, `enterkeyhint` and `spellcheck` of its host to the textarea.

## Implementation notes

- Both implementations share the anatomy and the stylesheet of the text fields with TextField; the web component also shares the controller of the value and the form, without implicit submission.
- With `resize="auto"`, each implementation measures the height of the content after each edit and after each change of width, and gives it to the stylesheet as a private property. CSS sizing by content is not yet available in every supported browser.

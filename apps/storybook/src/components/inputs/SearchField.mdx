# SearchField

<ComponentOverview name="SearchField" />

## Usage

Use SearchField for a query that filters or searches content. Put it in a form inside a `<search>` element, or an element with `role="search"`: the landmark belongs to the search form, not to the field. Use TextField for other single-line text.

## Anatomy

The **base** holds the **label**, **control** and **hint**, as in TextField. Inside control, **input-row** holds the decorative **leading-icon**, the native **input** and the **clear** button with its **clear-icon**. The web component exposes these parts; React uses `.ui-search-field__<part>` classes. In Figma, the component holds an exposed TextField: set its properties there, and show the clear button with its trailing icon.

## Variants & tone

<FrameworkCanvas react={ReactStories.Variants} webComponents={WebComponentStories.Variants} />

`outline` is the default, `filled` adds a filled surface and `underlined` keeps only the bottom border, as in TextField. There is no tone.

## Sizes & density

`small`, `medium` (the default) and `large` follow the sizes of TextField, and each follows the surrounding density.

## States

`state` is `default`, `error` or `success`, as in TextField. Error sets `aria-invalid` and the feedback colours; it does not create a native validation failure. Hover and press share the active styling, keyboard focus uses the focus roles, and disabled fields leave the tab order and submit nothing. Read-only fields stay focusable and submit their value.

## Content & labels

Supply a label, visible or as the `aria-label` of the author, in the language of the page. The field has no placeholder of its own; add one only as an example. The clear button is named by `clearLabel` or `labels.searchField.clear` (Clear search).

## Behaviour

<FrameworkCanvas react={ReactStories.InForm} webComponents={WebComponentStories.InForm} />

- The clear button shows only while the field holds a query that the user may change: never when it is empty, disabled or read-only.
- The clear button and Escape empty the field, keep the focus in the input and report two changes: the new empty value (`ui-value-change`, `onValueChange`) and the clearing (`ui-clear`, `onClear`). They dispatch no native `input`.
- Escape in an empty or read-only field does nothing and reaches the page, so a dialog around the field can close.
- Enter submits the owning form as in TextField. The value, reset, restore and validity follow TextField.

## Accessibility

- **Pattern.** A native search input, which the browser exposes as a searchbox, and a button after it. The page supplies the [search landmark](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/landmarks/examples/search.html).
- **Keys.** Escape clears the query; Tab moves from the input to the clear button; Enter submits.
- **Target.** The clear button keeps the icon size of the field and a pointer target of at least 24 by 24 pixels.
- **Preferences.** Forced colours keep the button visible and its focus indicator. The browser's own clear button is hidden, so there is one.

## Surfaces & themes

The field follows Light and Dark and the default, subtle, inverse and primary surfaces with the `control` family, as TextField does. The icons take the icon colour of the field; the button adds a tint on hover and press.

React's className, style and data-\* attributes reach the root; its ref, id, ARIA and native attributes reach the input. The web component uses the attributes `clear-label`, `label-placement`, `min-length`, `max-length`, `auto-complete` and `readonly`, and passes the native `inputmode`, `enterkeyhint` and `spellcheck` of its host to the input.

## Tokens

<ComponentTokens component="control" />

SearchField uses the `control` family of TextField, plus `color/control/action/background/active` and `control/action/target/min-size` for its button.

## Implementation notes

- Both implementations share the anatomy and the stylesheet of the text fields with TextField; the web component also shares the controller of the value and the form.
- React hides the clear button with its stylesheet while a disabled fieldset disables it, since a fieldset changes without a render.

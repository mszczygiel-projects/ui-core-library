# Popover

<ComponentOverview name="Popover" />

## Usage

Use Popover for a few controls or a short piece of content that a button opens beside it, such as the filters of a list or the settings of a view, while the page stays usable.

Use Tooltip for a short text without controls, Dialog for a decision that must stop the user, and Menu for a list of actions.

## Anatomy

The **base** holds the **trigger**, a button of the page, and the **panel**, which holds the content and, with `showArrow`, the **arrow**. The web component exposes these as parts and takes the trigger in its `trigger` slot; React takes it in the `trigger` prop and uses the matching `.ui-popover__<part>` classes. Figma names the layers the same way.

## Variants & tone

<FrameworkCanvas react={ReactStories.Placements} webComponents={WebComponentStories.Placements} />

`placement` sets the side of the trigger: `bottom`, the default, `top`, and `start` and `end`, which follow the direction of the text. The panel is centred on its trigger. When the window has no room on that side, it opens on the opposite one, and it always stays inside the window. `showArrow` adds an arrow that points at the middle of the trigger.

## Sizes & density

The panel is as wide as its content, up to the window. Density changes its padding.

## States

A popover is open or closed. The trigger shows its own states, and `aria-expanded` tells whether the panel is open.

## Content & labels

Nothing visible names the panel, so `label` is required. The trigger must act as a button: a Button, an IconButton or a native `<button>`. The content goes into the default slot, or `children`. Popover adds no text of its own, so it has no labels in `configureUiCore`.

## Behaviour

<FrameworkCanvas react={ReactStories.WithForm} webComponents={WebComponentStories.WithForm} />

- A press on the trigger opens and closes the panel. When it opens it, the focus moves to the element marked with `autofocus` or `data-autofocus`, else to the panel.
- Escape closes it and returns the focus to the trigger; inside a modal, Escape closes the popover first, and the modal stays open.
- A press outside the trigger and the panel, and the focus leaving both, close it without moving the focus.
- Each request carries a `reason`: `trigger`, `escape`, `outside` or `focus-out`. The web component dispatches the cancelable `ui-open-change` before it opens or closes; React reports `onOpenChange(open, details)` and follows `open` when the page controls it.
- Setting `open` does not report a request.

## Accessibility

- **Pattern.** The panel is a non-modal dialog, as in the [WAI-ARIA dialog pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/), and the trigger a [disclosure](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/disclosure/) button: it carries `aria-expanded` and `aria-haspopup="dialog"`.
- **Keys.** Enter and Space on the trigger open and close the panel; Tab moves through it and out of it, which closes it; Escape closes it.
- **Names.** `label` names the panel. Button and IconButton pass the state of the trigger to their native button.
- **Contrast.** The content keeps a contrast of at least 4.5 to 1 on the panel, in Light and Dark. In forced colours the panel keeps its border.

## Surfaces & themes

The panel, its border and its shadow follow the theme through the Components tokens of popover. The panel opens in the top layer and takes the surface of the page around its trigger.

## Implementation notes

- Both render the panel in place, with the native `popover="manual"` attribute, so that it opens in the top layer above every stacking context; Floating UI places it and keeps it in the window.
- The web component writes `aria-expanded` and `aria-haspopup` on the element of its `trigger` slot, and reflects only `open`. `ui-open-change` bubbles, so check `event.target` when a component inside the panel sends its own.
- React clones its `trigger` to add these attributes, a ref and a press, and keeps the ref and `onClick` of the page; an `onClick` that prevents the default keeps the panel as it is. The panel renders closed on the server, and `defaultOpen` opens it after hydration.
- The Figma set has the variant properties `open` and `showArrow`; `placement` and `label` live only in code.

# CheckboxField

<ComponentOverview name="CheckboxField" />

## Usage

Use CheckboxField for a choice a user turns on or off and confirms later with a form, such as accepting terms, or for each of several independent choices. Use SwitchField for a setting that applies at once, RadioGroup for one choice among several, and Chip for a compact filter.

## Anatomy

The **base** holds the **label** and the **hint**. The label holds the **control**, with the native **input** drawn as the box and the **mark** inside it, and the **label-text**. The hint holds the **hint-text**. The web component exposes these parts; React uses `.ui-checkbox-field__<part>` classes. Figma names the layers the same.

## Variants & tone

CheckboxField has one appearance. A checked box takes the colour of the primary action.

## Sizes & density

The box has one size, which follows the surrounding density.

## States

<FrameworkCanvas react={ReactStories.States} webComponents={WebComponentStories.States} />

- **Clear, checked and mixed.** The box draws the state of its native input: a check while checked and a dash in the mixed state of `indeterminate`.
- **`state`** is `default` or `error`. Error sets `aria-invalid` and the error colours of the box, label and hint; it does not create a native validation failure. A choice has no success state.
- Hover and press share the active styling, keyboard focus uses the focus roles, and a disabled box leaves the tab order and submits nothing.

## Content & labels

Supply `label` and `hint` in the language of the page. A box without a visible label takes its name from `aria-label`. CheckboxField renders no text of its own.

## Behaviour

<FrameworkCanvas
  react={ReactStories.Indeterminate}
  webComponents={WebComponentStories.Indeterminate}
/>

- A click on the box or its label, or Space, toggles the box and reports the new state once: `ui-checked-change` with `detail.value` in the web component, `onCheckedChange(checked, details)` in React, and a native `change` in both.
- The web component takes its initial state from the `checked` attribute; its property holds the current state. React offers `defaultChecked`, or `checked` with `onCheckedChange`.
- A click ends the mixed state, as on a native checkbox. A box that summarises others sets `indeterminate` again from their states.
- While checked and enabled, the box submits `value` (`on` by default) under `name`. `required` blocks the submission until the box is checked. A reset restores the initial state without an event and keeps the mixed state.

## Accessibility

- **Pattern.** A native [checkbox](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/checkbox/), with the mixed state of the tri-state pattern.
- **Keys.** Tab reaches the box, and Space toggles it.
- **Name and description.** The label names the box; the hint describes it and stays out of its name.
- **Target.** A box without a label keeps a pointer target of at least 24 by 24 pixels.
- **Preferences.** Forced colours keep the border of the box and its mark; the colours change without animation under reduced motion.

## Surfaces & themes

The box, label and hint follow Light and Dark and the default, subtle, inverse and primary surfaces. A checked box takes the primary action of its surface and draws its mark in the colour on it.

React's className, style and data-\* attributes reach the root; its ref, id, ARIA and native attributes reach the input. The web component is form-associated: it exposes `form`, `validity`, `validationMessage`, `willValidate`, `checkValidity()`, `reportValidity()` and `setCustomValidity()`, follows a disabled fieldset, and passes an `aria-label` of its host to the input.

## Implementation notes

- Both implementations draw the native checkbox itself, with `appearance: none`, so the drawing follows the state of the input in every mode.
- The web component shares the controller of its checked state, entry and validity with SwitchField. React renders the native input, so the browser submits and validates it.

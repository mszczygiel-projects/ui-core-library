# RadioField

<ComponentOverview name="RadioField" />

## Usage

Use RadioField only as an option of a RadioGroup, which holds the chosen value, the name, the validation and the state. Outside a group, an option has no state and submits nothing.

## Anatomy

The **base** holds the **label** and the **hint**. The label holds the **control**, with the native **input** drawn as a ring and a dot while the option is chosen, and the **label-text**. The hint holds the **hint-text**. The web component exposes these parts; React uses `.ui-radio-field__<part>` classes. Figma names the layers the same.

## Variants & tone

RadioField has one appearance. A chosen option takes the colour of the primary action.

## Sizes & density

The radio button has one size, which follows the surrounding density.

## States

<FrameworkCanvas react={ReactStories.Disabled} webComponents={WebComponentStories.Disabled} />

An option is chosen when the value of its group equals its `value`, and shows the error state of its group. `disabled` disables the option alone; the arrow keys skip it.

## Content & labels

<FrameworkCanvas react={ReactStories.WithHint} webComponents={WebComponentStories.WithHint} />

Supply `label` and `hint` in the language of the page. The label names the radio button and the hint describes it.

## Behaviour

The group handles the keys, the value and the form. See RadioGroup.

## Accessibility

A native radio button in the [radio group](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/radio/) pattern. Its pointer target is at least 24 by 24 pixels, and forced colours keep its ring and dot.

## Surfaces & themes

The option follows Light and Dark and the default, subtle, inverse and primary surfaces.

## API

<FrameworkTabs
  of={ReactStories.Playground}
  react={<ReactApi component="RadioField" />}
  webComponents={<WebComponentApi tag="ui-radio-field" />}

React's className, style and data-\* attributes reach the root; its ref, id, ARIA and native attributes reach the input. The web component passes an `aria-label` of its host to the input.

## Tokens

<ComponentTokens component="radio" />

The label, the hint and the spacing come from the `choice` family.

## Implementation notes

The web component tells its radio button its place in the group with `aria-posinset` and `aria-setsize`, since each radio button lives in its own shadow root.

# Tab

<ComponentOverview name="Tab" />

## Usage

Use Tab only as a direct child of Tabs, which renders its tab and shows its panel. Outside Tabs it shows its content as a plain block, without a role.

## Anatomy

A Tab holds the content of its panel. Tabs renders its **tab**, with the **tab-icon** and the **tab-label**, and the **panel** around its content, so the parts belong to Tabs. In Figma the component set Tab draws one tab in each variant, orientation and state of Tabs.

## Variants & tone

A Tab follows the `variant` and the `orientation` of its Tabs, which the Figma properties of the same names preview.

## Sizes & density

A Tab has one size, which follows the surrounding density through the tokens of Tabs.

## States

`disabled` keeps the tab from being selected and takes it out of the arrow keys. Whether a tab is selected is the `value` of its Tabs.

## Content & labels

`label` is the text of the tab, which names the tab and its panel. `icon` is a decorative icon before the label: an SVG string of the icon package in the web component, a React node in React. The content goes into the Tab as children or into its default slot, and stays in the page while another tab is selected.

## Behaviour

A Tab has no behaviour of its own: Tabs selects it, renders its tab again when `label`, `icon` or `disabled` changes, and reports the selection.

## Accessibility

Its tab is a `tab` of the [tabs](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/tabs/) pattern, named by `label`, and its panel a `tabpanel` named by its tab. Tabs handles the keys.

## Surfaces & themes

A Tab follows Light and Dark and the default, subtle, inverse and primary surfaces.
